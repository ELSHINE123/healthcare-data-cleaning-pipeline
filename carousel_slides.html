01 • THE PROBLEM

The Messy Reality of Raw EHRs

Real healthcare data is full of traps that break analytical models.

Composite Blood Pressure
Stored as raw unstandardized slash string; unusable for statistical math.
"120/80"
Verbal Words in Numeric Columns
Text values mixed with float decimals and null entries.
"forty"
Polymorphic Timestamps
Inconsistent delimiters and underscore formatting across clinics.
"2023_04_15"
THE CORE DANGER:
Blindly calling df.fillna() assigns artificial clinical profiles to blank records. You end up "inventing" fake patients.

03 • METHODOLOGY

Hierarchical Cascading Imputation

Why universal mean imputation is dangerous in healthcare.

A 25-year-old female and a 75-year-old male have completely different healthy vital baselines. Flat averages destroy this distinction.
TIER 1
(Age Group + Biological Sex) Subgroup Median
Most accurate clinical estimate based on demographic peer cohort.
TIER 2
Biological Sex Fallback Median
Applied only if age subgroup data is sparse.
TIER 3
Global Population Median
Final safety fallback ensuring zero unresolved values.
